# Work Spots — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-13_

Work Spots is a local-first app. **It collects no data.**

- No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
- Your check-ins, ratings, added spots and display name are stored **only on
  your device**, in the app's private storage. They are never transmitted to
  us or to any third party.
- The app makes no network requests of its own. Tapping a spot's map link
  opens Apple Maps / Google Maps search in your browser or maps app; that
  service's own privacy policy applies there.
- Deleting the app deletes all of its data. There is nothing on a server to
  delete, export or correct — because there is no server copy.

App Store privacy label: **Data Not Collected.**

Store privacy answers will be updated before that version ships._
